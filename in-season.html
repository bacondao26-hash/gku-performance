In-Season Program

2 strength maintenance sessions + 2 mobility/flexibility sessions per week. Intensity stays high, only volume drops — this is what keeps off-season gains from leaking away across an 18-week season.

Congestion rule

Two matches in one week? Drop to 1 strength session (Nordic + Copenhagen + one compound lift only) and keep both mobility sessions short. Never cut intensity to fit more volume in — cut volume, keep intensity, per the MED principle above.
