Off-Season Program

Triphasic block structure: Eccentric (Wk1-4) → Isometric (Wk5-8) → Concentric/Power (Wk9-12). Full weight room + field. No 1RM needed — loading is RPE-autoregulated throughout.

Deload week — volume down ~40-50%. Keep intensity feel light, movement quality high, and protect sleep. This is what makes next block's overload work.

RPE quick reference

RPE 5 = easy, could do 5+ more reps · RPE 6-7 = moderate, 3-4 reps left in tank · RPE 8 = hard, 1-2 reps left · RPE 9 = near-max, 1 rep left · RPE 10 = max effort. No tested 1RM needed — this scale self-corrects as you get stronger. Retest with the testing guide every 4-6 weeks to confirm progress.
