Off-Season Program

3 blocks: Eccentric (Wk1-4) → Isometric (Wk5-8) → Power (Wk9-12). No 1RM needed — RPE-based loading.

Deload — volume down ~40-50%, intensity stays. Protect sleep.

RPE quick reference

RPE 5 = easy, 5+ reps left · RPE 7 = 3 left · RPE 8 = 1-2 left · RPE 9 = 1 left · RPE 10 = max. Retest every 4-6 weeks.
